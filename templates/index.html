Roadmap

Stages 1 and 2 are live today. The later stages are planned and are not implemented yet.

Stage 1 · live

Voice sales assistant

Browser microphone input, spoken replies, call timer, live transcript, text fallback and server-side Gemini answers.

Stage 2 · live

Persistent call history

Every call is stored in SQLite on the server, so the transcript, duration, sales status and summary survive a restart.

Stage 2 · live

Summaries and sales status

An end-of-call summary of requirements, objections, next steps and outcome, plus a status you can set yourself: Converted, Interested, Follow-up Required, Not Interested, Demo Requested or No Response.

Stage 2 · live, with limits

Recording with consent

The customer is asked for permission before anything is captured and files stay on this computer. Only the customer microphone is captured — the browser cannot record the assistant's voice, so this is not a two-sided call recording.

Stage 3 · planned

Two-sided call recording

Capturing both the customer and the assistant on one file needs a server-side voice pipeline, not just the browser microphone.

Stage 4 · planned

Pipeline and reporting

Grouping calls into a sales pipeline, filtering the history and exporting results for the team.
